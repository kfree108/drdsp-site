What it is, plainly

The Trade Desk describes itself on its own site as the leading independent DSP built for data-driven marketers, spanning connected TV, display, native, video, audio, mobile and digital out-of-home, with retail media data available alongside. DSP here means Demand-Side Platform — the buying software — and not Delivery Service Partner, Amazon's delivery-van franchise that unhelpfully shares the letters.

The word doing the work in that description is independent. The Trade Desk sells no media of its own. It does not own the inventory it buys, which means its incentives point at getting the buyer a good price rather than at filling its own supply. In a market where the largest platforms are vertically integrated, a genuinely buy-side-only vendor is a structural position, not a slogan.

This review is written by Dr. DSP, a managed Amazon DSP product from Full Circle, a full-service Amazon management company: $500M+ in managed spend across 100+ brands. We do not compete with The Trade Desk for most budgets, and where we do, the honest answer is often theirs.

What it is genuinely excellent at

  • Connected TV. This is the strongest argument for the platform. Premium streaming inventory bought programmatically, at scale, with frequency managed across publishers instead of wasted separately inside each one.
  • The open internet. Breadth of supply beyond the walled gardens, with the tooling to buy it deliberately rather than by accident.
  • Identity work. UID2 is a serious attempt at addressability that does not depend on third-party cookies, and it has industry adoption behind it rather than being a single vendor's private scheme.
  • Supply path. OpenPath and the surrounding supply-path optimisation work exist to remove intermediaries between the buyer and the publisher. That is the kind of unglamorous plumbing that quietly returns money to advertisers.
  • Independence as governance. For agencies managing many clients, a buy-side-only platform is easier to defend in a procurement review than one that also sells the media.

If your growth question is reach — audiences that have never heard of you, on screens Amazon does not own — this is a capable answer and the shortlist is short.

The honest limitations for an Amazon-first advertiser

Not flaws. Boundaries, and they matter enormously depending on where your revenue comes from.

  • Inventory access is the real moat in 2026. The most valuable streaming and retail inventory increasingly sits behind platform-specific access, and no single DSP has all of it. Which inventory a given DSP can buy changes with commercial deals, so verify the current position with the vendor rather than trusting a comparison article — including this one.
  • Amazon retail signal is Amazon's. Buying against Amazon shopping behaviour, and measuring against Amazon purchase events, happens inside Amazon's own stack.
  • Access is usually via an agency. If that is the route you take on the Amazon side, our own agency arm reMKTR buys Amazon DSP on its own seats as a managed service, while Dr. DSP is the same capability sold as a product your team directs — worth knowing which of the two you actually want before you shortlist anyone. Most brands do not sit directly in the platform. That is a fine model, but it adds a layer whose fee and cadence you should specify.
  • No published price. Their /pricing URL returned a 404 when we checked on 20 August 2026. Cost is a platform fee agreed in contract as a percentage of media. We cover the structure on the pricing page.

The measurement point, which is the whole reason this page exists

If most of your revenue is transacted on Amazon, the hardest question in display is not which platform buys the impression. It is whether the impression changed anything.

Last-click attribution cannot prove incrementality and never could. It reports which touch happened last. A shopper who sees your ad, then searches your brand, then buys, hands the credit to search. A retargeting impression served to someone already on their way back gets credited with a sale that was already yours. Every platform in this market reports its own contribution, and adding two reports together produces a business bigger than the one your accounts show.

What resolves it is a control group — a holdout on a matched region or ASIN set — and a place where impressions and purchases can be compared at event level. On Amazon that place is Amazon Marketing Cloud, Amazon's clean room, where DSP and sponsored ads stop double-counting each other and you can ask what the combination did that neither did alone. That reconciliation is native to buying through Amazon DSP.

None of which makes The Trade Desk a worse platform. It makes it a platform pointed at a different question. If your growth is off-Amazon, their measurement partnerships and your own incrementality testing are the right tools. If your growth is Amazon, buy where the clean room is.

How to choose between an independent DSP and Amazon DSP

Answer four questions and the decision usually makes itself.

  • Where does the revenue land? If it is overwhelmingly Amazon, start where the purchase data and the clean room are. If Amazon is one of several channels, an independent DSP earns its place.
  • What is the objective? Reaching people who have never met your brand, at scale, on television-grade inventory is The Trade Desk's home ground. Converting shoppers already in an Amazon buying journey is not.
  • Who operates it? A platform is a console. Someone has to run it daily, in-house or at an agency, and the cost of that person is part of the comparison.
  • How will you prove it worked? Whatever you choose, agree the test before the budget. If a vendor will not design a holdout, or tells you a platform report is proof, keep asking.

Plenty of brands should run both, with different jobs assigned to each and no expectation that the two reports will reconcile on their own.

Which one you should actually pick

The Trade Desk is the right platform for brands and agencies buying open-internet and connected-TV reach at scale, where independence, identity and supply-path control genuinely matter. An Amazon-first brand whose real question is whether display added sales should buy where the clean room is — Amazon DSP, with the holdout designed before the budget.

What to do with this

Judge this on the job you actually need done, not the feature list. Pull your own search-term report for the last 90 days and total the spend against terms that produced no orders — on the account above that was 33.6% of everything spent. Then ask whether the thing you are about to buy closes that gap, or just shows it to you.

Common questions

Is The Trade Desk good for Amazon sellers?

It is good at reaching people who are not currently shopping you — awareness, CTV, open-web video. What it cannot do is reconcile impressions against Amazon purchase events inside Amazon's clean room. If Amazon is where your sales happen, that reconciliation is usually worth more than incremental open-web reach.

Can I run The Trade Desk and Amazon DSP together?

Yes, and many advertisers should. Give each a distinct job — reach and frequency on one, shopping-signal retargeting and prospecting on the other — and do not expect their reports to add up. Two platforms each claiming the same conversion is the double-counting problem in its purest form.

What do reviewers criticise about The Trade Desk?

The consistent themes are a steep learning curve and the total cost once data, measurement and agency fees are stacked on the platform fee. Both are consequences of an enterprise product built for professional traders rather than a self-serve tool, and neither is a defect for the buyer it was built for.

Does The Trade Desk publish pricing or minimums?

No. The pricing URL returns a 404 and the site invites you to make contact. The commercial shape is a platform fee as a percentage of media, agreed in contract, with data and measurement passed through. Ask for the percentage and the tier that produced it, in writing.

How do I test whether display is working at all?

Withhold it. Pick a matched set of regions or ASINs, hold display out for a defined window, and compare against the control while reconciling impressions and orders at event level. Design the test before the budget is agreed. If a partner resists that conversation, you have learned something valuable at no cost.
